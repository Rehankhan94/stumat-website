Research across disciplines and institutions.

STUMAT combines a food-packaging researcher's experience with expertise in bio-based chemistry, polymer materials and microencapsulation.

RK
MSCA Postdoctoral Fellow

Dr. Muhammad Rehan Khan

I work at the intersection of food science and packaging materials. My research focuses on the design and evaluation of materials that can help protect food and better understand changes during storage.

Before STUMAT, I trained in Food Science at the University of Naples Federico II and conducted research on bio-based food contact materials, release behaviour and food-quality indicators. The fellowship at URD ABI in France extends this work into ferulic-acid-derived additives and smart PHBV films.
