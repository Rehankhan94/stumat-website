Why STUMAT?

A material with promise. A performance challenge.

Poly(3-hydroxybutyrate-co-3-hydroxyvalerate), or PHBV, is a bio-based polymer with potential for food packaging. Its brittleness and other performance limits make wider use difficult.

STUMAT investigates whether ferulic-acid-derived additives can tune PHBV films, and whether encapsulated Spirulina extracts can provide a visible response to changing food conditions.

How it works

Four connected research activities

The project follows a pathway from additive design through encapsulation and film formulation to a food model.

✳
01 · Tune PHBVDesign bio-based additives and study their interactions with the polymer.
◎
02 · Make microbeadsEncapsulate Spirulina-derived colour compounds in alginate.
▱
03 · Build smart filmsCombine tuned PHBV films with the microbeads and study release.
◒
04 · Test the responseCompare film colour, pH and spoilage measures in a model food.
