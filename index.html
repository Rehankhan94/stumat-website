BIO-BASED FILMS × RESPONSIVE INDICATORS
The idea

Can food packaging do more with less?

PHBV is a promising bio-based polymer, but its mechanical performance can limit its use in food packaging. STUMAT aims to tune PHBV films with ferulic-acid-derived additives and explore colour-changing Spirulina extracts as indicators of food spoilage.

Research approach

From molecules to smart films

01 / DESIGN

Improve PHBV

Develop bio-based additives and study how they interact with PHBV to tune film properties.

02 / ENCAPSULATE

Protect the indicator

Use droplet millifluidics to encapsulate Spirulina-derived extracts in alginate microbeads.

03 / TEST

Explore food applications

Integrate these components into films and investigate their potential for spoilage detection.
