Dissemination

Sharing findings with the research community

As results become ready, STUMAT aims to share methods and findings with researchers and professionals.

01 / PUBLICATIONS

Peer-reviewed research

Planned journal articles cover the material design, smart-film approach and related review work. Papers will be listed here after publication.

02 / CONFERENCES

Scientific exchange

Presentations and posters are envisaged at meetings relevant to food science, packaging, bioeconomy and materials research.

03 / OPEN SCIENCE

Reusable outputs

The project plans to share appropriate publications, methods and data through suitable open repositories, subject to research and intellectual-property requirements.

Communication

Making the science understandable

Communication explains the question and why it matters to people outside the laboratory. It can use plain-language stories, visuals and demonstrations to show how a packaging film is designed and evaluated.

PROJECT SITE

Research in plain language

This website introduces the project and can grow with confirmed news, images and accessible explanations.

PUBLIC STORIES

Short updates

Planned social media updates, local articles and a leaflet will explain project aims and later findings.

A question to explore

What can a film tell us about food quality?

Visual explanations and future demonstrations can connect material properties, a natural colour indicator and the measurements needed to evaluate it.

Claims about food-safety performance will only be made after testing and validation.

Outreach & knowledge exchange

Meeting students, researchers and the public

EDUCATION

Students

Planned lectures, school materials and undergraduate presentations introduce sustainable materials and experimental research.

PUBLIC EVENTS

Science festivals

Planned outreach formats include events such as Fête de la Science and European Researchers' Night for public engagement.

COLLABORATION

Workshops

Seminars and webinars provide space to discuss methods and exchange expertise across the host and partner teams.

TWO-WAY LEARNING

Training

The fellowship brings together green chemistry, microencapsulation, modelling and food-packaging knowledge.

These activities are planned. This page does not announce confirmed event participation or completed outputs.
